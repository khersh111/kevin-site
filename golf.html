Your body shouldn’t be your handicap.

Remote coaching for golfers whose body is costing them distance, rounds and their best golf, from an orthopedic PT and former college golfer.

The problem

Your body is setting the limits on your swing.

Stiffness or pain in your back, hips, shoulders or elbows limits your swing. You're losing distance, your warm-ups keep getting longer, and some rounds you cut short.

Swing coaches know the swing but not the body. Therapists know the body but not the swing. Most golfers are stuck between the two.

Coaching that looks at your swing AND your body.
