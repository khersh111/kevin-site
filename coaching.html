Train with a physical therapist in your corner.

Most people build their training from podcasts, programs, and whatever they saw last week. This is the opposite. One plan—lifting, cardio, and mobility—built for your body and your goals, adjusted every week, overseen by a fellowship-trained PT. So you stop guessing and start building toward the decades ahead.

Who this is for

This is for you if you're in your 20s, 30s, 40s, or 50s and you've decided your body is worth taking seriously now—not after something breaks. You train, or you're ready to start. You want to be carrying your own bags and getting off the floor without a second thought at 70, living independently well into old age.

You don't want a generic program pulled off a shelf. You want someone who is a movement expert and knows how the body works, building the plan, watching your form, and adjusting course when something isn't working.

It's not for you if you're after a quick fix, you won't put in the work between sessions, or you need someone to keep you motivated. I build the plan and keep it honest. The training is yours to do.

What you get each month

  • A program built for you, not pulled off a shelf. Lifting, cardio, and mobility in one coherent plan, built around your goals, your schedule, and where your body actually needs the work.
  • Weekly check-ins. Every week we review how training went, what felt good, what didn't, and what changes next. You're never grinding through a stale program for months.
  • Async form reviews. Send video of your lifts. I review your technique and send back specific corrections—so small faults get caught before they become the thing that sidelines you.
  • A monthly video call. Once a month, we talk through how you're feeling, what's working, and what the next block looks like.
  • Direct access. Message me with questions between sessions. You've got a fellowship-trained PT to answer whatever questions you have about your body or training.
