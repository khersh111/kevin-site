How I think about training

Your program is built on one idea.

Most training only adds load—more weight, more miles, more sessions. Whether your body turns that into strength or breakdown comes down to three things: how much your tissue can handle, how well you move under load, and how fast you add it.

That's what I build your program around. Not just the lifts you want to hit, but the capacity underneath them, the movement patterns that decide where force actually goes, and a rate of progress your body can absorb. It's the difference between training that works for a season and training that holds up for decades.
