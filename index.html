The approach
What decides whether training breaks you down or builds you up.

Training adds load: more weight, more miles, more sessions. Whether your body turns that into strength or injury comes down to a few things.

01

Capacity

How much your tissue can handle before something gives. Tendons and joints adapt slower than muscle, so they're usually the weak link, but they're trainable.

02

Movement quality

Move well and load spreads across your tissues the way it's built to. Move poorly, rep after rep, and the same load keeps concentrating in the same spots.

03

Load management

Most injuries in healthy people aren't from doing too much. They're from adding too much too fast. Knowing how to progress is a skill.

None of it is dramatic. It's the boring stuff that compounds, which is why most people skip it, and why it works.
